There is not always one reason behind the problem. Your internet connection, Wi-Fi signal, streaming device, IPTV player, and the individual stream can all affect playback.

2. Restart Your Router and Streaming Device

If your streaming device is far away from your router, a weak Wi-Fi signal may cause Sky Glass IPTV buffering.
Modern routers often offer two Wi-Fi bands:
2.4 GHz: Offers better coverage over longer distances but can experience more interference.
5 GHz: Often provides faster speeds and less interference at shorter distances, but has a shorter range.
If your streaming device is reasonably close to the router, try connecting it to the 5 GHz network.
